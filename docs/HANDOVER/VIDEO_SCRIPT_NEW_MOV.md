# Voiceover Script — RPD Portfolio walkthrough (`new.mov`, 7:07)

Narration timed to the screen recording `~/Desktop/new.mov` (2866×1678, no audio track).
Timestamps are from the recording. Pace is about 140 words a minute. Each voiceover block fits
inside its window with a little room to spare. Figures are the ones visible on screen in the demo
data set (46 projects, planning week W31).

**Tone:** calm, confident and plain. Speak to Frigoglass R&D leadership. Explain *why* a screen
matters, not just *what* is on it.

## Production notes

- **Total voiceover:** about 930 words. That fits 7:07 at a natural pace with pauses on the KPI
  reveals.
- **Hold points:** pause on the KPI cards (0:05), both agent answers (1:50, 2:55), the "How this
  is calculated" panel (3:35) and the auditor lock screen (6:45).
- **Suggested on-screen captions:** "11 within year · 24 spillover · 10 left out" (0:08);
  "Financials & names never sent to the AI" (1:45); "Client workbook formulas, shown in full"
  (3:35); "Append-only audit log" (6:10).
- **Demo-mode caveat:** the login page shows "Test logins … RPD_DEV_MODE". If this video goes to
  the client, say once that this is the demo environment and that production uses SSO (covered
  in §13).
- **Two things on screen worth fixing before a re-record:**
  1. The agent's answers show raw `**bold**` markdown and mention "the JSON". The panel should
     render markdown, and the prompt should say "the project data".
  2. On the test-login card, *Dave ExecutiveViewer* is labelled "Engineer".
