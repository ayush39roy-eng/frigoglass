# Video script: prioritization matrix + dynamic planning (real 2026 data)

About 1:45 long, around 260 words. Read the scores and weeks off the live screen.

**0:00–0:20 · Matrix: open "FV1000sz Heineken 2026 Cladding"**
"Every 2026 project is scored on 13 dimensions in five pillars: strategy, regulation, finance,
market and volume, and investment. Each is scored 1 to 5 against a written rubric, and each pillar
has its own weight. Investment is inverted, so a cheaper project scores higher."

**0:20–0:40 · Same project: score panel and band**
"The weighted total becomes a percentage. Seventy and above is P1, fifty-five is P2, forty is P3,
and anything lower is P4. This Heineken cladding project is Marketing's number one, and it sits
in P1."

**0:40–0:55 · Stay on the score panel**
"Some things can't wait for a score. A regulatory deadline, a customer certification at risk, or a
safety issue forces a project straight to P1."

**0:55–1:20 · Priority board, P1 and P3 columns**
"Here is the Priority board. In P1 are Marketing's top asks: the Heineken cladding and the twelve
Frameless Lookalike Door cooler variants, from S-35 to S-1100. Down in P3 is Drawer Freezer
Climate ST, an OEM project planned in Europe. Watch: I drag it up to P1."

**1:20–1:40 · Timeline updated panel**
"That's dynamic planning. The move re-plans the portfolio by itself, right away. The drawer freezer
now gets its engineer and its Greece lab chamber ahead of P2 and P3 work. The panel shows its new finish week and exactly which
projects slipped to make room."

**1:40–1:50 · Hold on the panel**
"Score, prioritise, re-plan. One drag, and everyone sees the consequence."

Note: say "dynamic planning" on camera, not "dynamic programming". The engine is not dynamic
programming.
